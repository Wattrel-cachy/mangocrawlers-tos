Mango Crawlers VR – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Mango Crawlers VR ("the game") is a small multiplayer VR game made by an independent developer ("I", "me"). This page explains what data the game collects, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: I collect only what's needed for online play, I don't use ads or analytics, and I never sell your data.

What the game collects

DataWhy
Anonymous account ID: a one-way hash (SHA-256) made from an ID your headset gives the game. The original hardware ID never leaves your device, and the hash can't be turned back into it. So you keep the same name and stats every time you play, and so bans work.
Player name: either one assigned at random (like "Troll1234") or one you pick yourself. Shown to other players in the game.
Basic play stats: when you first and last played, how many times you joined, and total play time. Running and moderating the server.
Live game data: your head/hand positions, gun actions, health, and in-game events (like who hit whom). Sent to other players in real time so multiplayer works. Not stored, apart from the server logs below.
Voice: if you allow microphone access, your voice is sent live to nearby players while you're talking. Voice chat. Your voice passes through the server but is never recorded or stored.
Server logs: player names, join and leave times, renames, in-game kills, and moderation actions. Fixing bugs and dealing with cheating or abuse.

The game does not collect your real name, email, Meta account details, location, contacts, or payment information. It also has no ads, analytics, or tracking.

The game also saves your settings (like graphics options) on your headset only. They are never sent anywhere.

Microphone

The game asks for microphone permission for voice chat. You can refuse it or turn it off any time in your headset settings (Settings > Privacy > App permissions > Microphone). You can still play and hear others without it.

Who else sees your data

  • Other players see your name, avatar movements and actions, and hear your voice.
  • Cloudflare carries connections to the game server, so it handles your IP address when you connect. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. The game server itself does not log IP addresses.
  • Meta runs the Quest platform and store. Meta's own handling of your data is covered by Meta's privacy policy.

I don't sell, rent, or share your data with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

How long data is kept

  • Your account record (ID, name, stats) is kept while the game is online, or until you ask me to delete it.
  • Server logs are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Ban records are kept for as long as a ban lasts.

Your rights and deleting your data

You can ask me to show you, correct, or delete your data at any time. Email mangocrawlers@proton.me with your in-game player name. I'll reply within 30 days. Once your data is deleted, you'll start over with a new random name next time you play. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU or UK), you may have extra rights under laws like the GDPR, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The game is not made for children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you're a parent or guardian and think your child has played, email me and I'll delete their data.

Security

Connections to the game server are encrypted (WSS/TLS). Server data is stored on a private server that only I can access.

Changes

If the game starts collecting anything new, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
